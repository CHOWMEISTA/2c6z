The Rules

This is semi-anarchy. Know where the line is drawn before you play.

  • Allowed: Raiding, griefing, stealing, and ruthless PVP anywhere in the world.
  • Banned: Hacked clients (X-ray, fly, killaura, speed), duping, and intentional lag machines.
  • Chat: Keep it competitive, but don't dox players or break basic platform TOS.
